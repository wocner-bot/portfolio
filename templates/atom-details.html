ATOM / ELECTRIC VEHICLE

One coherent
in-car experience.

The work brought together the assistant’s interaction logic, conversational flows and sound feedback, with consistent behavior across key in-car scenarios.

It also covered the e-Mirror interaction model and the Smart Widget Panel — distinct parts of the wider cockpit experience.

01 / AI ASSISTANT

From activation
to action.

Interaction architecture, conversational UI and contextual quick actions for vehicle functions.

Activate & listen

Pressing the assistant button opens the overlay in a listening state, ready for a command. The same button or Back closes it.

Contextual quick actions

Suggested actions include opening seat-heating settings and turning on ventilation. Frequent actions appear beside the conversation.

Conversation & feedback

Prompts, intents and conversational flows work with the assistant’s tone of voice and audio identity.

02 / E-MIRROR

A new view
behind the wheel.

An interaction model for a camera-based mirror with multiple viewing scenes and adjustable framing.

Clear activation

An illuminated frame confirms that the mirror is on. The frame then fades, leaving the camera image.

Scene switching

The interaction model supports parking, highway, urban and custom camera views.

Adjustable view

The driver can change the scene angle, adapting the camera framing to the situation and their preference.

03 / SMART WIDGET PANEL

Vehicle controls,
close at hand.

A contextual home screen and a two-layer favourites panel with quick access to climate and vehicle controls.

Quick climate controls

Temperature, fan and airflow controls sit together in the first layer of favourites.

Two layers of shortcuts

A swipe reveals another layer of vehicle controls, keeping the initial panel focused.

Defined transitions

The interaction specification covers opening favourites, switching layers and returning to the home screen.
